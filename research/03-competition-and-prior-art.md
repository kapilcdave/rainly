# Competition and prior art

## Who's already here

| Who | What they do | Why it isn't Rainly |
|---|---|---|
| **Blanket** (tryblanket.app, public Aug 2026) | You describe a risk; it suggests Kalshi contracts, models cost vs payout, and flags weak matches. Built by economist Lauris Zminsky with Kalshi staff via the Builders Program. | Advice only: no execution, holds no funds. Its own "Gaps" page lists **weather** as a risk with no good matching market. |
| **Sensible Weather** | Rain guarantees at checkout, at 450+ KOA campgrounds, 120,000+ Hipcamp sites, and Tentrr. Pays automatically by text, no claim. 1M+ guarantees sold through 8,000+ partners (company figures, 2025); attach rates reported up to 5× travel insurance. Example: $12.78 on a $115 site night (~11%), paying 100% after 3+ hours of rain. | Sells to big travel platforms; backed by insurance capital, not Kalshi. |
| **Vortex RainSure** (Aug 2025) | Parametric rain insurance for businesses, paying from 0.01 inch, backed by Mitsui Sumitomo, measured by WeatherWorks. | $250 minimum premium, $10k–$500k limits: too big for a taco truck with a $600 Saturday. (Also why the app isn't called RainSure.) |
| **28 Wishes** (LA ice cream shop) | The owners trade Kalshi temperature contracts themselves; sales drop ~20% below 70°F; they report ~$1,500/month from trades. | One customer trading on its own, not a product. Hedges temperature, not rain. |
| **Discrete** (Houston) | Did Kalshi's first privately negotiated weather block trade: $25k paying if Houston hits 103°F in August. | Energy-retailer hedging, large tickets. |
| **Aviva Canada** | Rain-day policy for roofers; pays when rain days beat the site's average. | Insurance product, Canada, roofers only. |
| **Adaptive Insurance** | Parametric weather cover for businesses. | Insurance, not checkout refunds. |
| **WeatherBill** (2007) | Tried selling weather hedges to small businesses; became The Climate Corporation, pivoted to farming, sold to Monsanto. (From background knowledge, not the searches.) | Proof the market was tried before the right instruments existed. |

## The gap Rainly fills: ticket size

Insurance products start at hundreds of dollars of premium with five-figure limits. Kalshi lets you sell a **$2 refund to one customer for one Saturday**, hedged exactly, and that only became possible in 2026, when daily rain markets launched. Nobody sells Kalshi-backed rain refunds to local businesses' customers.

## Why the competition is good news

- Sensible's 1M+ guarantees prove customers pay for rain refunds.
- Blanket proves Kalshi wants apps like this and backs them through its Builders Program.
- Kalshi already markets "small businesses hedge weather," so judges may have heard the idea.

## Market context

- Kalshi weather volume up ~500% year over year, on pace for ~$1.1B/year (Kalshi, Aug 2026).
- Sports is still about half of Kalshi's volume, so weather is small by comparison.
- The Weather Company shows Kalshi's live weather probabilities to some weather.com and app users.
- Kalshi's Builders Program ($2M) pays apps a share of fees on the volume they bring.
